Frequently asked questions

How do I book tickets?

Pick a movie, choose a date, cinema and showtime, select seats, add snacks if you like, then pay. Your ticket appears under My Bookings.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. You can cancel up to 2 hours before the show. The ticket amount is refunded; the convenience fee is not.

Where is my ticket?

Log in and open My Bookings. Show the barcode at the cinema entrance, or print the ticket.

Is my payment real?

No. This is a demo checkout and no money is taken. Connect a payment provider before going live.
